The canopy uses one photographed common-fig leaf (*Ficus carica*), masked from the surrounding foliage. Martin Vorel publishes the photograph as CC0 / public domain on LibreShot: [Common Fig Leaf](https://libreshot.com/common-fig-leaf/) (`https://libreshot.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/fig-leaf.jpg`). The files in this repo are a cropped alpha mask of that photograph. The underside, normal, roughness, and thickness maps are derived from the same photo. LibreShot states the picture may be used commercially without attribution; attribution is recorded here anyway.

Files live under `assets/fig-tree/`.
