The Stoning — Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

This policy applies to the iOS game The Stoning (STONED).

Information collection and use

STONED runs on your device and does not require an account. The app does not collect or send personal information, include advertising or analytics, or track you across apps or websites.

Contacting the developer

If you choose to contact the developer outside the app, the information you share will be used to respond to your request. You may contact the developer through the project support page. Please do not post private information in public issues.

Apple and website services

Apple and GitHub may process information independently when you use their services, including the App Store or this hosted policy page. Their own privacy policies apply to those services.

Changes to this policy

If the app's privacy practices change, this policy will be updated.
